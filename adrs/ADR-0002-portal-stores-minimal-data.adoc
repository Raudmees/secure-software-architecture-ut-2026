= ADR-0002: The portal stores minimal data; agencies and registries keep business data
:toc:

== Status

Accepted

== Context

The portal sits between residents and the agencies and registries that deliver public services.
We must decide where business data lives: case content, decisions, documents, profile data and
delegations. A wrong boundary could require major architectural redesign later
(R13 in link:../risk/risk_register.adoc[the risk register]).

== Decision

The portal stores as little business data as possible.

* Case content, decisions, documents, profile data and delegations stay with the agencies and
  registries that own them. The portal reads them live through the Agency integration container.
* The portal stores only:
** preferences, catalogue configuration, API client registrations, sessions, the notification inbox and submission records (Portal DB (minimal));
** files in transit, until they are scanned and delivered (Temporary file store);
** queued requests to agencies (Message broker);
** the audit trail (Audit store).
* Agency staff never use the portal. The portal sends requests and documents to the agencies'
  own systems (Agency back-end e-services), and agency staff work there.

== Alternatives considered

* *Central portal store*: the portal stores case content and documents centrally, and agency
  staff process cases in the portal's back-office. 
* *Hybrid*: the portal stores documents but not case content. Not chosen because documents are
  among the most sensitive data the portal handles, and a case and its documents would then live
  in two different systems of record.

== Consequences

* Positive:
** The portal holds less personal data, so a breach of the portal exposes less (QA-2).
** The agency keeps the official version of its own cases, decisions and documents
** There is no second copy of agency data to keep in sync.
* Negative or risks:
** The portal depends on agency availability for live data: when an agency system is unavailable
   or slow, requests cannot be delivered and live case status cannot be shown (R15).
** Queued requests in the Message broker contain personal data, so the broker must be encrypted,
   access-controlled and durable.
** Document access by agency staff happens in agency systems, so it does not appear in the
   portal's audit trail or in the resident's data-usage view. This limits transparency (BG-3).
* Follow-up actions:
** Add R15 to the risk register.
** Show the data ownership in link:../docs/architecture.adoc[architecture.adoc] section 4.
