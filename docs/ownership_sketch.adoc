= Ownership Sketch
:toc:

Initial ownership assumptions for the containers in
link:architecture.adoc[architecture.adoc] (section 4). Teams follow the Team Topologies
types. These are assumptions for a real implementation and may change.

== Ownership

[cols="2,1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Team |Type |Containers owned

|Portal Services Team
|Stream-aligned
|Citizen web app, back-office web app, Portal backend (catalogue and preferences, cases,
documents, signing workflow, notifications)

|Identity and Access Team
|Complicated-subsystem
|Identity & access

|Agency Integration Team
|Stream-aligned
|Agency integration, API gateway (public and provider APIs, versioning)

|Audit and Transparency Team
|Complicated-subsystem
|Audit service, audit store

|Platform and Operations Team
|Platform
|Message broker, Portal DB (minimal), Temporary doc store, antivirus scanner, observability
|===

The Platform and Operations Team runs the shared stores and the broker; the team that writes
a given kind of data owns its schema and retention rules.

== Conway check

* *Portal Services and Identity and Access:* every protected operation depends on
  authorization decisions. Reduced by a stable, versioned authorization API owned by
  Identity and Access.
* *Portal Services and Agency Integration:* requests and documents cross to agencies through
  the integration service. Reduced by the canonical data model, so backend modules do not
  depend on agency-specific formats.
* *All teams and Audit and Transparency:* every container must emit consistent audit events.
  Reduced by a shared audit event schema and client library published by the audit team.
* *All teams and Platform and Operations:* shared broker and databases. Reduced by
  self-service provisioning, so teams do not wait on tickets.

Earlier versions split the backend between a Citizen Services Team and a Case, Document and
Signing Team. After the backend became one container, that split would have made two teams
coordinate on every release of one deployable, so the teams were merged.
