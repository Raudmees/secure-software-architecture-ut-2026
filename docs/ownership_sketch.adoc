:toc:
:toclevels: 2

== Conway Check

=== Citizen Services Team (stream-aligned team)

Owns the citizen-facing portal, service catalogue and the main citizen service/case workflows (end-to-end).

=== Identity and Access Team (complicated-subsystem / enabling team)

Owns the integration with the national eID/OIDC provider, authentication sessions and common authorization mechanisms.

A coordination hotspot exists between this team and every service that needs authentication or authorization.

=== Agency Integration Team (stream-aligned team)

Owns the public/provider APIs, integration adapters and onboarding mechanisms for agencies and external registries.

API changes and schema versioning are likely coordination hotspots.

=== Case, Document and Signing Team (stream-aligned / complicated-subsystem team)

Owns case-management functionality, document exchange and digital-signature integration.

Coordination with the Agency Integration Team is required where cases or documents must be transferred to external agencies.

=== Audit and Transparency Team (complicated-subsystem team)

Owns the common audit event model, audit-log collection and storage, and functionality that allows citizens and auditors to see how personal data has been accessed.

A coordination hotspot exists because all services processing citizen data must produce consistent audit events.

=== Platform and Operations Team (platform team)

Owns shared deployment infrastructure, monitoring, logging, tracing, secrets/configuration management and common resilience mechanisms.
The platform should provide these capabilities to the service teams through standardized and preferably self-service mechanisms.
