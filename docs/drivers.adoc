== Mission goals

The refined business goals for the Citizen Services Portal. Quality attribute scenarios in
link:qa_scenarios.adoc[qa_scenarios.adoc] refer to these goals.

[cols="1,5",options="header"]
|===
|ID |Business goal

|BG-1 |Provide residents with a single trustworthy digital entry point through which they can discover, request, track, and manage public services.
|BG-2 |Ensure that citizen data is processed lawfully, securely, and only for appropriate purposes.
|BG-3 |Allow residents and authorized oversight bodies to understand and verify how personal data and public-service requests have been accessed and processed.
|BG-4 |Allow new public agencies and services to join the portal without major changes to either the portal or existing agency systems.
|BG-5 |Ensure residents can reliably access essential portal capabilities despite component failures or temporary problems in external systems.
|BG-6 |Make public services understandable and usable by residents with different languages, abilities, ages, and levels of technical literacy.
|BG-7 |Allow the portal to evolve as public services, regulations, integrations, and technologies change without requiring major system redesign.
|===

== Out of scope

* The portal integrates with national eID/OIDC identity providers but does not issue or manage national identity credentials.
* Browser/device compromise is outside the portal's direct control, although the portal should still use appropriate session and authentication protections.
* The portal integrates with email/SMS/push providers but does not control the security or availability of the telecommunications infrastructure itself.

== Stakeholders

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Stakeholder |Need or concern

|Residents/citizens (diagram: Resident / Citizen) |A single, simple, localized way to find and use public services, confidence their data is handled safely and only used for stated purposes, visibility into who accessed their data and why.
|Agencies (diagram: Agency staff, Agency back-end e-services) |An easy way to integrate their own services and systems into the portal without needing to completely rebuild current solutions.
|Administrators/helpdesk (diagram: Administrator / Helpdesk) |Role-based access to case data and citizen support tools, limited only to what their role needs.
|Auditors/oversight (diagram: Auditor / Oversight body) |Complete logs of who accessed what data, when, and why, across the whole system.
|External registries/back-end systems (diagram: External registries, Agency back-end e-services) |Predictable and well-documented APIs, backend needs to be sure that frontend will not be overloading it with requests.
|Operations/security teams (diagram: Operations / Security team) |Observability into system health and security events.
|Third-party service providers (diagram: Third-party service providers) |A stable, documented and versioned public API with clear authorization scopes, so their integrations do not break.
|===

== Assumptions

* The country in which and for what the system will be designed is Estonia.
* A national eID or another identity provider exists and is available for the portal to delegate authentication to.
* Back-end e-services expose APIs that the portal can integrate with.
* Agencies are willing to adopt our API/schema defined by the portal.
* Residents without eID can still reach public services through non-digital channels (for example service offices). Whether the portal must also offer a digital fallback is open (R14).
* Peak load clusters around known deadlines (for example the tax declaration period). Size is not yet known (see R11).

== Open questions

. Which specific services and agencies will be integrated?
. How many active users are in the country the system will be built?
. What are the expected peak load scenarios the system must be designed to handle?
. Which data should the portal store and process itself, and which should remain with the agencies? Decided in link:../adrs/ADR-0002-portal-stores-minimal-data.adoc[ADR-0002].
. Is there a legally required fallback authentication method for residents who cannot use the national eID?
