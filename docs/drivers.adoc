= Architectural Drivers
:toc:
:toclevels: 2

== Mission goals

List 5-8 goals that explain what the Citizen Services Portal should achieve.

* Ensure the system meets legal and regulatory obligations for privacy and data protection.
* Provide auditors and oversight bodies with the ability to verify that citizen data is accessed and processed lawfully.
* Provide citizens the ability to see for what and how is their personal data used (transparency).
* Build a single trustworthy entrypoint for all services, replacing current fragmented portals and processes.
* Enable new agencies and services to be onboarded onto the portal with minimal integration effort.
* The system should be able to handle technical errors to maintain 24/7 availability.


== Stakeholders

Identify stakeholders and what each needs from the architecture.

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Stakeholder |Need or concern

|Residents/citizens |A single, simple, localized way to find and use public services, confidence their data is handled safely and only used for stated purposes, visibility into who accessed their data and why.
|Service provider organizations/agencies |An easy way to integrate their own services and systems into the portal without needing to completely rebuild current solutions.
|Administrators/helpdesk |Role-based access to case data and citizen support tools, limited only to what their role needs.
|Auditors/oversight |Complete logs of who accessed what data, when, and why, across the whole system.
|External registries/back-end systems |Predictable and well-documented APIs, backend needs to be sure that frontend will not be overloading it with requests.
|Operations/security teams |Observability into system health and security events.
|===

== Quality attributes and constraints

List 8-12 quality attributes or constraints.

[cols="1,3,3",options="header"]
|===
|ID |Driver |Why it matters

|QA-001 |Must comply with GDPR (or local equivalent) and national eID/trust regulation |Non-compliance risks legal penalties and loss of public trust.
|QA-002 |Availability and resilience |The portal is critical public infrastructure, citizens must be able to access core functions at all times.
|QA-003 |Role-based access control for “other” users |Administrators, helpdesk, and agency staff need different, limited views into the system.
|QA-004 |Privacy by design |Each service and agency must only see the data necessary for its specific purpose.
|QA-005 |Fast and easy onboarding of new agencies  |The portal's value depends on how easily new services and agencies can be added.
|QA-006 |Observability (metrics, logs, tracing) |Operations and security teams need to detect service levels faults and security incidents quickly. They would also trace the system for any criminal activity.
|QA-007 |Usability and localization |Citizens span multiple languages, ages, and technical literacy levels. The interface, notifications, and service catalogue must be understandable and accessible to all residents.
|QA-008 |Citizen authentication via federated identity (eID/OIDC) |The portal must not manage citizen credentials itself, so authentication is delegated to a trusted national identity provider.
|===

== Assumptions

List assumptions that may later need validation.

* The country in which and for what the system will be designed is Estonia.
* A national eID or another identity provider exists and is available for the portal to delegate authentication to.
* Back-end e-services expose APIs that the portal can integrate with.
* Agencies are willing to adopt our API/schema defined by the portal.
* Citizens without eID access will have some alternative path into the system, even if not the primary one.
* Traffic patterns are largely predictable, with known peak periods.

== Open questions

List open questions as questions, not as hidden assumptions.

* Which specific services and agencies will be integrated?
* How many active users are in the country the system will be built?
* What are the expected peak load scenarios the system must be designed to handle? 
* Are there data residency or storage restrictions that would prevent the portal from centrally storing certain agencies' documents or case data?
* Is there a legally required fallback authentication method for residents who cannot use the national eID
