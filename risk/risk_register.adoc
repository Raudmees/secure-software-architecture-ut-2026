= Risk Register
:toc:

[cols="1,4,1,1,4",options="header"]
|===
|ID |Risk |Likelihood |Impact |Mitigation / treatment

|R-001
|If traffic is higher than expected, then services will be unavailable.
|Low
|High
|Load testing, capacity planning, rate limiting, graceful degradation of non-critical functions.

|R-002
|If stored case, document or audit data volumes grow larger than expected, then the system will become unavailable.
|Low
|High
|Use of X-Road instead of storing all data in-house, scalable storage, retention/archiving policies, storage monitoring and realistic volume tests. +
Mindset: store as much data as necessary, but as little as possible.

|R-003
|If an external agency changes its API, then integrations will fail and services will become unavailable.
|Medium
|High
|Where possible, use X-Road to learn about upcoming API changes in advance.

|R-004
|If an external agency or registry is unavailable, then integrations will fail and services will become unavailable.
|Medium
|High
|Timeouts, circuit breakers, bounded retries, asynchronous processing, graceful degradation of broken functions.

|R-005
|If a user's authentication session is compromised, then an attacker may access personal information or perform actions under another person's identity.
|High
|High
|Strong federated multi-factor authentication (Smart-ID, ID-card), short-lived sessions, step-up authentication for sensitive actions.

|R-006
|If roles or authorization policies are configured incorrectly, then staff or agencies may gain access to citizen data beyond what their role or purpose requires.
|Medium
|High
|Centralized authorization rules, least privilege, audit logging of access decisions.

|R-007
|If an unauthorized party gains access to stored personal data, then citizen privacy may be breached and the system may violate data-protection obligations.
|Medium
|High
|Privacy by design, data minimization, pseudonymization, access controls, secure secret/key handling, retention rules.

|R-008
|If security and data-access events are not logged completely or audit records can be altered, then citizens and auditors may be unable to verify how personal data was accessed and processed.
|Medium
|High
|Centralized structured audit events, immutable storage, correlation IDs, restricted access to logs.

|R-009
|If major dependency updates occur, then the system may fail.
|Medium
|Medium
|Very high integration test coverage.

|R-010
|If our portal's API changes, then existing integrations with third-party service providers will break.
|Medium
|High
|API versioning strategy.
|===

== Notes

* Keep risk statements concrete: "If _condition or event_ happens, then _impact_ occurs."
* Track owners and dates in repository issues or in this document.
